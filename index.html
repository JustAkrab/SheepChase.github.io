The one job

SheepChase is a survival game with a single rule: keep your distance from the sheep for as long as you can.

The flock never stops coming. You walk the field, read where the sheep are heading, and stay out of reach. The run lasts until they catch up.

WASD

Walk. Eight-direction movement across the field.

Left click

Attack. The swing animation is in; hit detection and damage are still being built.

Level Design

Build log

Player movement

Done

WASD walking with a character that turns to face the direction of travel.

  • Movement driven by a CharacterController & Rigidbody
  • Speed tuning for walking and turning designed by C# scripts.
  • Camera follow setup to follow the player character

Sheep pursuit

Done

Every sheep tracks the player and closes the gap.

  • How the sheep find you: NavMesh agents, with 2d collision circles that show where the person is.
  • How the flock closes in without bunching up: 2d collision capsules designed to add a physical feel to the sheep, hence avoiding clustering or overlap.
  • Balancing sheep speed by using a set speed amount.

Attack animation

Done

Left click triggers the attack clip through the Animator.

  • Added Animator trigger fired on left click
  • Designed Blend trees between idle, walk, and attack.
  • Utilized C# scripts to design player combat logic.

Takeaways

Scope small, ship it

One rule and two inputs was enough to get a playable build online and in front of players.

Pursuit needs tuning
